# Code of conduct

Diorama is built by and for people who study how coding agents behave. Everyone who takes part (in issues, pull requests, discussions or anywhere else the project is represented) is expected to help keep it a place where people want to contribute.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and assume good intent. Disagree with ideas, not people.
- Be patient with newcomers and with questions that have been asked before.
- Give and take feedback on the work, constructively.
- Respect people's privacy: don't share someone else's personal information, sessions, captures or code without their permission.

## What isn't acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or demeaning comments about anyone's identity or background.
- Sexualized language or imagery, threats, or encouraging others to do any of this.
- Using Diorama, or the project's spaces, to deceive or harm other people.
- Repeatedly pushing a conversation after being asked to stop.

## Enforcement

The maintainer ([@ridvanaltun](https://github.com/ridvanaltun)) may edit or remove comments, issues and code that break these rules, and may block people who keep breaking them. To report a problem, contact the maintainer privately through GitHub. Reports are handled confidentially.

This code applies to every project space, and to public spaces when someone is representing the project.
